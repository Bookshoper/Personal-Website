Natural Lakeside Cinematic Photo Style Skill

Version: 1.0 Author: Richard Liang Start Date: 2026.08.01 Language: Natural language Status: Completed

本 Skill 致力于将图片转换为具备自然湖畔、旅行摄影与慢生活氛围的真实电影感摄影作品。其核心并非虚构无中生有的场景，而是在保留原始真实性的前提下，强化自然光影、空间层次与真实摄影质感。

核心美学理念 (Core Identity)

"Quiet Nature, Slow Living, Cinematic Reality"

  • 情绪关键词：安静、自然、温暖、松弛、旅行感、生活感、真实摄影
  • 适用场景：自然风景（湖泊、山林、草地）、户外生活（木质建筑、咖啡馆、露营区）及旅行纪实照片

Anti-AI 真实摄影法则 (Anti-AI Rules)

  • 拒绝过度完美：拒绝区域全清与色彩过艳，保留自然虚化、景深重点与真实瑕疵
  • 坚持留白空间：预留天空、水面与阴影等空旷区域，严禁填满画面
  • 视觉元素克制：遵循“1 个主要视觉故事 + 1 个辅助元素”法则，避免多主体竞争焦点
  • 真实相机质感：模拟手机/35mm胶片旅行摄影的自然曝光变化，严格禁止 CG 渲染感、游戏截图及商业广告味

三层空间与色彩控制

  • 构图重塑：建立 前景（树枝/栏杆） → 中景（主体/人造结构） → 远景（山体/水面/天空） 的纵深关系
  • 色彩纯化：天空保持自然柔和蓝、水面呈现蓝绿层次反射、木质结构强化温润纹理，严禁荧光色与 HDR 效果

完整 Skill 规则文档与评估代码 (System Specification)

# Natural Lakeside Cinematic Photo Style Skill
Version: 1.0 | Author: Richard Liang | Date: 2026.07.02

## 1. Core Style Identity
"Quiet Nature, Slow Living, Cinematic Reality"
Keywords: peaceful, relaxing, natural, quiet, slow life, authentic photography.

## 2. Anti-AI Photography Rules
- Avoid Artificial Perfection: Preserve defocus areas, organic flaws, and optical depth.
- Preserve Empty Space: Maintain uncrowded areas (sky, water, shadows).
- Limit Visual Elements: 1 Main Story + Max 1 Secondary Element.
- Real Camera Feeling: Emulate 35mm travel film and natural mobile lens exposure.

## 3. Image Analysis & Spatial Depth
- Composition Target: Foreground -> Subject -> Background (3-Layer Depth).
- Lighting: Maintain natural golden/morning/afternoon scattering light.
- Texture: Enhance wood bark, timber grain, and water ripples with subtle film grain.

## 4. Output Self-Check System (Quality Score Formula)
Final Score = (Realism * 0.30) + (Color * 0.20) + (Atmosphere * 0.25) + (Composition * 0.10) + (Detail * 0.15)

Pass Condition: Final Score >= 8.0
If Final Score < 8.0: Automatically adjust parameters based on the lowest sub-score.
